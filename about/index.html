About Us

Comfort Specialists for the Festival City & Perth/Huron

From heritage homes along the Avon River to farmhouses tucked between Mitchell and Wingham, we keep Stratford and the surrounding county comfortable through humid lakeside summers and long, snowy winters.

Our Story

Built Around Stratford's Real Seasons

Living and working here means we understand exactly how Lake Huron shapes our weather: sticky July afternoons, heavy lake-effect snow, and shoulder seasons that flip almost overnight.

Stratford Heating & Cooling was started to bring honest, neighbourly service to a community we genuinely love. Our technicians live locally and understand the quirks of everything from century Victorians near downtown Stratford to newer builds in the city's growing subdivisions, so every repair, install, or tune-up is matched to your actual home rather than a generic checklist.

As a family-operated company serving the Festival City and the wider Perth/Huron region, we treat each call the way we'd treat one for our own family. That means straightforward pricing before we begin, careful workmanship while we're there, and follow-up you can rely on long after the truck pulls away.

We also know that comfort out here isn't one-size-fits-all. A drafty farmhouse near Seaforth has different needs than a bungalow in Listowel or a downtown Stratford walk-up, and we bring that local judgment to every furnace, air conditioner, heat pump, and ductless system we touch.

Our Commitment

A Different Kind of HVAC Company

We service the full mix of equipment found across Stratford, Mitchell, Seaforth, Goderich, Listowel, Clinton, and Wingham, from high-efficiency furnaces and central air conditioners to ductless mini-splits, cold-climate heat pumps, smart thermostats, and professional duct cleaning that clears out a season's worth of dust and allergens.

Whether you're calling about a furnace that quit before a lake-effect cold snap, a ductless install in a home without existing ductwork, or a thermostat upgrade to rein in rising energy bills, our certified technicians bring the same careful, transparent approach to every visit, day or night.
